Q: Which eMTB motor has the most torque?
A: The Bosch Performance CX produces 85Nm of torque, matched by the Yamaha PW-X3 at 85Nm and the Specialized S3 Full Power at 90Nm. Q: Is Bosch or Shimano better for electric mountain bikes?
A: Neither is universally better — they suit different priorities. Bosch Performance CX delivers higher torque (85Nm) and broader dealer support; Shimano EP801 is quieter, slightly lighter and integrates seamlessly with Shimano drivetrains. Q: How much does an eMTB motor weigh?

Q: What is the DJI Avinox motor?
A: DJI Avinox is a mid-drive eMTB motor system developed by DJI — better known for drones — offering 105Nm peak torque and integrated smart features including automatic mode selection. It currently powers the Amflow PL Carbon (M1) and Amflow PX Carbon (M2S), both stocked at Peak Pedal.

Q: Can I replace my eMTB motor if it breaks?
A: Yes — mid-drive motors on established platforms (Bosch, Shimano, Yamaha) are serviceable through authorised dealers across the UK. Motor units can be replaced if damaged beyond repair, though this is uncommon. Contact us on WhatsApp for advice on specific model servicing options.

**Definition Hook:** "Electric mountain bikes are legal in the UK when they comply with EAPC (Electrically Assisted Pedal Cycle) regulations: motor rated at no more than 250W continuous power, assistance cutting out at 15.5mph, and the rider being at least 14 years old. All bikes stocked by Peak Pedal are EAPC-compliant."

**FAQ:**
Q: Can I ride an electric mountain bike on UK trails?
A: Yes, on any trail that permits regular mountain bikes. eMTBs are classified as bicycles under UK law when EAPC-compliant, so they are treated identically to unassisted mountain bikes for trail access purposes. Always check individual trail centre rules, as some privately managed venues have their own policies.

Q: Do I need insurance for an electric mountain bike in the UK?
A: No — EAPC-compliant eMTBs do not require insurance, registration or a licence to ride in the UK. You may choose to add cycling insurance for theft or personal liability cover, but it is not a legal requirement.

Q: What is the minimum age to ride an electric mountain bike in the UK?
A: 14 years old is the minimum age to ride an EAPC-compliant electric mountain bike on public roads and trails in the UK. There is no upper age limit.
