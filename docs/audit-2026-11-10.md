## 3. AI visibility

- Present: `llms.txt` follows the llmstxt.org format, with brand facts and contact details. AI bots are allowed in `robots.txt` and the Content-Signal directive is set. FAQPage schema is on the home, blog and FAQ pages. `speakable` is on the homepage. Organization/Store schema carries `areaServed`, `knowsAbout`, `priceRange` and `makesOffer`. ChatGPT already cites 6 prompts and Gemini 1.
- **A1 (High, needs your decision): the homepage `aggregateRating` (4.8 from 2,418 reviews) and the on-page "Trustpilot-style" reviews.** The data was migrated from a previous site (recorded in `PROJECT.md`; the client confirmed it) but is not verified against a live Trustpilot or other third-party profile. The review dates are static ("2 days ago"), and one review names a model, "Cube Stereo Hybrid 140 HPC SLX 750", that isn't in the current catalogue. The brand is only about 4 months old online and nothing outside the site corroborates the count. Google also treats a business rating on its own site as self-serving and doesn't show it as a rich result, so it adds risk with little upside. Recommendation: confirm the source with the client; if it can't be verified on a public profile, drop the aggregate rating from the schema and the "Trustpilot" branding.
- A2: `sameAs` is empty and there is no `foundingDate` or `@id` on the Organization schema. These are correct, because no real profiles or history were supplied and nothing may be invented. Getting real profiles live (Google Business Profile, social, directories) is what unlocks them.
- A3: `Article` author is Organization-only (correct, since no named author was supplied). Blog dates are present.
- A4: `robots.txt` still lists outdated or incomplete bot names: `Claude-Web` and `anthropic-ai` are retired; `Claude-User`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Perplexity-User`, `MistralAI-*` and `Meta-WebIndexer` are missing. `ai-train=no` differs from the skill default of `ai-train=yes`; that is a policy choice for you.
- A5: `llms.txt` shows the contact email in plain text (project rule says entity-encode emails everywhere).
- A6: Bing's new AI Performance report (Copilot citations) is available in the Bing tab and hasn't been checked yet.

## 5. Root cause

The site is technically sound and indexed. It ranks at ~44 because it has no genuine authority: the 176 referring domains are spam (99% authority score 0-10), and Bing and GSC both report no real inbound links. More on-page work will not move the average position. Link building and real brand profiles will, and so will pages that earn clicks from the queries it already shows for.
